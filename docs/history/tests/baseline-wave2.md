# RED baseline — wave 2 — answers from memory, 2026-10-09 (no tools, no skill)

1. **DESI DR1 peculiar velocities.** Maximum-likelihood fields fσ8 ≈ 0.48 ± 0.08; density–velocity/momentum power spectrum ≈ 0.44 (+0.08/−0.10); correlation functions ≈ 0.39 ± 0.08. Consensus fσ8 = 0.450 ± 0.055 at z_eff ≈ 0.07, DESI BGS density × FP+TF velocities, ΛCDM template with free fσ8. Velocity counts: roughly 7×10^4 Fundamental Plane and 7×10^3 Tully–Fisher (I do not remember the exact numbers). Source: DESI DR1 PV paper set, arXiv December 2025, around 2512.032xx (exact IDs not recalled). Confidence: medium on consensus and z_eff, low on per-estimator values, counts and arXiv ID.

2. **CF4 bulk flow.** Watkins et al. 2023 (arXiv:2302.02028): 419 ± 36 km/s at 200 h⁻¹ Mpc and 427 ± 37 km/s at 250 h⁻¹ Mpc, minimum-variance estimator, ΛCDM (Planck) expectation; first quoted as ~4–5σ excess (probability ~10⁻⁶–10⁻⁷). Whitford, Howlett & Davis 2023 (arXiv:2306.11269): different estimator/effective depth and larger errors (≈ 408 ± 165 km/s near 170 h⁻¹ Mpc), tension reduced to ~2–3σ, not significant. Latest 2026 reanalysis: I cannot recall a specific paper; my belief is that 2026 work (including DESI PV bulk flow) finds consistency with ΛCDM, but this is a guess. Confidence: medium for 2023 values, low for 2026.

3. **Evolving dark energy.** DESI DR2 BAO + CMB (arXiv:2503.14738), w0waCDM vs ΛCDM: 3.1σ without SNe; +Pantheon+ 2.8σ; +Union3 3.8σ; +DESY5 4.2σ (March 2025). After the DES-Dovekie recalibration (arXiv:2511.07517) the DES-SN combination drops to ≈3.2σ. DES Y6 all-probes (3×2pt + SN + BAO + clusters, arXiv:2601.14559, January 2026) with CMB: preference for w0wa of ≈3σ (I recall 3.2σ; low confidence). Bayesian evidence: weak to moderate at best, ln B of order 0–3 depending on SN sample and prior width; inconclusive or mildly favouring ΛCDM with Pantheon+; never decisive. Confidence: high for DR2 significances, medium for Dovekie, low for DES Y6.

4. **Modified gravity.** DESI DR1 full shape (arXiv:2411.12022): DESI alone μ0 = 0.11 (+0.45/−0.54); DESI + CMB (no lensing) + DES Y3 3×2pt + SNe: μ0 ≈ 0.04 ± 0.22, Σ0 ≈ 0.044 ± 0.047, consistent with GR; I do not recall a DR2 full-shape update. E_G: Wenzl et al. 2024 (arXiv:2405.12795), ACT DR6 + Planck lensing × BOSS: E_G = 0.31 (+0.06/−0.05) at z ≈ 0.555 versus GR 0.401 (CMASS); 0.49 (+0.14/−0.11) versus 0.452 (LOWZ). Hu–Sawicki: tightest overall |f_R0| < 1.4×10⁻⁸ from galaxy morphology (Desmond & Ferreira, arXiv:2009.08743); tightest cosmological log10|f_R0| < −5.3 from SPT clusters (Vogt et al. 2024). Confidence: medium; low on "latest".

5. **Sum of neutrino masses.** DESI DR2 BAO + CMB: Σmν < 0.064 eV (95%) in ΛCDM, < 0.16 eV in w0waCDM (arXiv:2503.14738; Elbers et al., arXiv:2503.14744; March 2025). Effective mass allowing negative values: Σmν,eff ≈ −0.10 (+0.05/−0.06) eV, about 3σ below the oscillation minimum 0.059 eV. Tightest published: Wang, Mena, Di Valentino & Gariazzo 2024, Σmν < 0.043 eV (95%) from CMB + DESI + SNe + other background data, ΛCDM (arXiv ID not recalled, ~2405.xxxxx); ACT/SPT + DESI DR2 combinations give ≈ 0.05–0.06 eV. Confidence: high for DESI limits, medium for effective mass, low for the tightest-limit attribution.

6. **Ordering and laboratory limits.** JUNO first result (59 days of data, arXiv:2511.14593, November 2025): sin²θ12 = 0.3092 ± 0.0087, Δm²21 = (7.50 ± 0.12)×10⁻⁵ eV²; no mass-ordering determination yet (needs ~6 years for ~3σ). KATRIN: m_ν < 0.45 eV at 90% CL from 259 days (arXiv:2406.13516, Science 2025); design goal 0.3 eV. Cosmological Bayes factor for normal over inverted ordering: of order 10:1 with DESI DR2 + CMB in ΛCDM, strongly prior-dependent (range ~3 to >30 in the literature; Gariazzo et al. versus Jimenez et al.), and it disappears in w0waCDM. Confidence: high for KATRIN, medium for JUNO numbers, low for the Bayes factor.

7. **Warm and fuzzy dark matter.** Lyα forest: m_WDM > 5.7 keV (95%), Iršič et al. 2024 (arXiv:2309.04533), high-resolution spectra; earlier > 5.3 keV (Iršič 2017; Palanque-Delabrouille 2020); > 3.1 keV (Villasenor 2023). Milky Way satellites: > 6.5 keV, DES + Pan-STARRS, Nadler et al. 2021 (arXiv:2008.00022). Strong lensing: > 5.2 keV (Gilman et al. 2020); JWST flux ratios > 6.1 keV (Keeley et al. 2024, arXiv:2405.01620); lensing + satellites > 9.7 keV (Nadler et al. 2021, arXiv:2101.07810). Fuzzy DM from the forest: m > 2×10⁻²¹ eV (Iršič et al. 2017, arXiv:1703.04683); > 2×10⁻²⁰ eV (Rogers & Peiris 2021, arXiv:2007.12705). Confidence: medium-high.

8. **Cluster cosmology.** eRASS1 (Ghirardini et al., arXiv:2402.08458): S8 = 0.86 ± 0.01, ΛCDM, weak-lensing mass calibration. SPT (Bocquet et al., arXiv:2401.02075): S8 = 0.795 ± 0.029, σ8 = 0.817 ± 0.026, DES + HST lensing. DES Y3 clusters (2025, arXiv ID not recalled): S8 ≈ 0.86 ± 0.04, much higher than Y1's 0.65 ± 0.04 (arXiv:2002.11124); low confidence. KiDS AMICO: DR3 S8 = 0.78 ± 0.04 (Lesci et al., arXiv:2012.12273); KiDS-1000 ≈ 0.86 ± 0.03 (2025; low confidence). Planck PSZ2 reanalysis (Aymerich et al. 2024, arXiv:2402.04006, Chandra-calibrated scaling relation): S8 ≈ 0.78 ± 0.02, 1−b ≈ 0.84 ± 0.04. Confidence: high for eRASS1 and SPT, low for the rest.

9. **CMB lensing tomography.** unWISE × ACT DR6 (Farren et al., arXiv:2309.05659): S8 = 0.813 ± 0.021; with Planck PR4, 0.810 ± 0.015. DESI LRG × ACT DR6 + Planck PR4 (Sailer et al., arXiv:2407.04607; Kim et al., arXiv:2407.04606): S8 = 0.765 ± 0.023 (BAO prior, ΛCDM). Earlier values: unWISE × Planck 0.784 ± 0.015 (Krolewski et al. 2021); DESI LRG × Planck 0.73 ± 0.03 (White et al. 2022). Reasons for the rise: spectroscopically calibrated redshift distributions, corrected lensing Monte Carlo normalisation/transfer function for the masked cross-correlation, improved magnification-bias and imaging-systematics treatment, updated lensing maps (PR4, DR6) and more conservative perturbative modelling/scale cuts. Confidence: medium-high for values, medium for causes.

10. **kSZ, DESI × ACT DR6.** Stacking: photometric LRG × ACT DR6 13σ (Hadzhiyska et al., arXiv:2407.07152); spectroscopic DESI DR1 LRG ≈ 9.8σ (Ried Guachalla et al., arXiv:2503.19870); I do not recall the DR2 numbers precisely (≳10σ, low confidence). Velocity reconstruction: ≈7σ with b_v ≈ 0.45 (+0.06/−0.05), below unity (Laguë et al., arXiv:2411.08240). Debate: gas profiles are far more extended than dark matter and than TNG-like models, favouring strong feedback (FLAMINGO strong-feedback variants, McCarthy et al., arXiv:2410.19905) and suppressing S8-relevant power; against, X-ray gas fractions and tSZ/eROSITA calibrations prefer milder feedback, and the kSZ inference depends on satellite fraction, halo mass and two-halo modelling. Confidence: medium-low.

11. **Beyond two-point.** Parity-odd 4PCF, BOSS: Hou, Slepian & Cahn (arXiv:2206.03625) 7.1σ in CMASS, 3.1σ in LOWZ; Philcox (arXiv:2206.04227) 2.9σ; later work (Krolewski et al., arXiv:2407.03397; Philcox & Ereza with GLAM mocks) attributes it to covariance mis-estimation, no robust detection. DESI DR1: an LRG measurement in 2025 (Slepian et al.) reports an excess whose significance depends strongly on the covariance; not established (low confidence). DESI bispectrum: adding it to the full-shape fit leaves the w0wa preference essentially unchanged or mildly stronger (Chudaykin, Ivanov & Philcox, arXiv:2507.13433). Voids: Nadathur et al. 2020 (arXiv:2008.06060), BOSS+eBOSS; Hamaus et al. 2020 (arXiv:2007.07895), Ωm = 0.312 ± 0.020. Confidence: medium, low on DESI.

12. **Field-level and SBI.** Parameters from data: SimBIG on BOSS CMASS — P_ℓ: Ωm = 0.292 (+0.055/−0.040), σ8 = 0.812 ± 0.067 (arXiv:2211.00723); field-level CNN: Ωm = 0.267 ± 0.03, σ8 = 0.762 ± 0.035 (arXiv:2310.15256); KiDS-1000 SBI S8 = 0.731 ± 0.033 (arXiv:2404.15402); DES Y3 mass-map SBI S8 = 0.794 ± 0.017 (Jeffrey et al., arXiv:2403.02314); KiDS-1000 CNN S8 ≈ 0.78 ± 0.06 (Fluri et al., arXiv:2201.07771); velocity–density forward likelihood fσ8 = 0.401 ± 0.024 (Boruah et al. 2020). Fields or simulation tests only: BORG on 2M++/SDSS (arXiv:1806.11117), CSiBORG, Manticore; LEFTfield σ8 gains (Nguyen et al., arXiv:2403.03220); BORG-WL and BORG-PV (arXiv:2204.00023) on mocks. Confidence: medium.
