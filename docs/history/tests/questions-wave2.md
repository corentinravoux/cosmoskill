# Test questions — wave 2 domains

Answer each with: the value(s) and uncertainties, the dataset and model
assumed, the arXiv ID of the source, and a confidence (high / medium / low).
If a question asks for "latest", state the date of the result you give.

1. DESI DR1 peculiar velocities: fσ8 from each of the three estimators, the
   consensus value and its effective redshift, and how many Fundamental Plane
   and Tully–Fisher velocities enter the measurement.
2. Cosmicflows-4 bulk flow: the amplitude and depth of the claimed excess, the
   significance as first quoted and as reassessed, and the latest (2026)
   reanalysis.
3. Evolving dark energy: the current significance of the w0wa preference for
   DESI DR2 with the CMB and each supernova sample, the DES Y6 all-probes
   combination, and what the Bayesian evidence says.
4. Modified gravity: the latest μ0 and Σ0 from DESI full shape with external
   data, the latest E_G measurement against its GR prediction, and the
   tightest bound on Hu–Sawicki f_R0.
5. Sum of neutrino masses: the DESI DR2 + CMB limit in ΛCDM and in w0waCDM,
   the effective (negative) neutrino mass result, and the tightest published
   limit with its authors.
6. Neutrino mass ordering and laboratory limits: the JUNO result, the KATRIN
   limit, and the cosmological Bayes factor for the normal ordering.
7. Warm and fuzzy dark matter: thermal-relic mass limits from the Lyα forest,
   Milky Way satellites and strong lensing, and the fuzzy-dark-matter mass
   limits from the forest.
8. Cluster cosmology: S8 from eRASS1, SPT, DES Y3 clusters, KiDS AMICO and the
   latest Planck PSZ2 reanalysis, and the hydrostatic mass bias the last one
   finds.
9. CMB lensing tomography: S8 from unWISE × ACT DR6 and from DESI LRG × ACT
   and Planck, and why both rose from the earlier, lower values.
10. kSZ with DESI DR2 and ACT DR6: detection significances, the velocity
    bias b_v, and the two sides of the baryonic-feedback debate.
11. Beyond two-point clustering: the status of the parity-odd four-point
    function in BOSS and in DESI, the effect of the DESI bispectrum on the
    w0wa preference, and the reference void–galaxy AP/RSD constraint.
12. Field-level and simulation-based inference: which analyses have produced
    cosmological parameters from survey data (give the values), and which
    have produced only reconstructed fields or simulation tests.
