# Test questions — wave 3 and wave 4 domains

Answer each with: the value(s) and uncertainties, the dataset and model
assumed, the arXiv ID of the source, and a confidence (high / medium / low).
If a question asks for "latest", state the date of the result you give.

1. Spectral index and tensors: the current values of n_s by CMB data
   combination, the latest limit on the tensor-to-scalar ratio and the
   experiment it comes from, and whether a newer B-mode result exists.
2. Primordial non-Gaussianity: the latest Planck f_NL values (local,
   equilateral, orthogonal), and the DESI f_NL from scale-dependent bias with
   the assumption it rests on.
3. Cosmic birefringence: the latest joint measurement of the angle β, its
   significance, and what its authors conclude.
4. Evidence for evolving dark energy with DESI DR2 and the CMB alone: the
   frequentist significance, the Bayes factor from an independent analysis,
   and why the two disagree.
5. Covariance from mocks: with 1000 mocks, by how much does the parameter
   error bar grow for 100 and for 400 data points, and which corrections
   are involved.
6. Simulation accuracy: the agreement between N-body codes on P(k), the
   accuracy of COLA with 40 time steps, and the baryonic suppression of P(k)
   at k = 1 h/Mpc inferred from data.
7. Constrained simulations of the local Universe: what the latest
   2M++-based reconstruction finds about a local underdensity, and the volume
   and resolution of its extension to the SDSS and BOSS volumes.
8. Facilities: the public Euclid releases with their dates and the planned
   ones, the latest public DESI spectra, and what eROSITA DR2 contains.
9. Cosmic dipole: the significance of the CatWISE quasar number-count dipole
   as first quoted and with clustering-aware mocks, and the result of the
   DESI redshift-dipole measurement.
10. Anomalies and the Hubble tension: the joint significance claimed for the
    large-angle CMB anomalies and its reassessment, and the range of the
    Hubble-tension significance according to the early-universe endpoint.
11. Reionisation and 21 cm: the range of τ from Planck low-ℓ polarisation,
    the HI intensity-mapping auto-spectrum detections, and the deepest 21 cm
    power-spectrum limits at z > 6.
12. Standard sirens: the latest LVK value of H0 with the catalogue and the
    number of sources used, the range of H0 values from GW170817 with
    jet information, and the completeness distance of GLADE+.
13. Conventions: how the σ8 of DESI DR1 full shape differs from Planck's,
    the order of the Limber error and the argument it requires, the
    growth-index formula with its accuracy, and the Planck 2018 values of
    the sound horizon at the drag epoch.
14. Codes: what CAMB v2 changes and its reference, what succeeds EFTCAMB,
    and the units flip uses for k, distances, angles and velocities.
