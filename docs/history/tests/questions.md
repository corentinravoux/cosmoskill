# Test questions — wave 1 domains

Answer each with: the value(s) and uncertainties, the dataset and model
assumed, the arXiv ID of the source, and a confidence (high / medium / low).
If a question asks for "latest", state the date of the result you give.

1. DESI DR2 BAO: with which combinations of external data is evolving dark
   energy (w0wa) preferred over ΛCDM, and at what significance for each
   supernova sample? Give w0 and wa for one combination.
2. DESI DR2 Lyman-α forest BAO: the precision of the measurement, the
   effective redshift, the values of D_H/r_d and D_M/r_d.
3. DESI Lyα P1D: which data releases have a published P1D, with which
   estimators, over which k and z ranges? Give the arXiv IDs.
4. ACT DR6 power spectra: H0 and ns in ΛCDM (ACT alone and ACT combined with
   Planck/WMAP), and the arXiv ID.
5. SPT-3G D1: H0 from SPT alone and from the SPT+ACT+Planck combination, and
   the quoted tension with SH0ES.
6. DES Y6 3×2pt: S8 in ΛCDM, and the level of agreement with the CMB.
7. KiDS-Legacy cosmic shear: S8, and the level of agreement with Planck.
8. DES-SN5YR: the number of SNe Ia in the Hubble diagram, the w constraint in
   flat wCDM, and what changed in the 2025–2026 recalibration of the sample.
9. Pantheon+ and Union3: the number of SNe in each and the Ωm each gives in
   flat ΛCDM.
10. The latest H0 from SH0ES and from CCHP (JWST), with the indicators used.
11. Euclid: which data releases are public as of October 2026, and the dates.
12. List the systematics a referee expects to see treated in (a) a cosmic-shear
    analysis and (b) a Lyα 3D BAO analysis, with one reference paper for each
    systematic.
