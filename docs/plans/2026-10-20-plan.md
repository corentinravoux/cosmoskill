## Aim

A general observational-cosmology skill package, useful to any researcher,
with four levels (L1 `SKILL.md`, L2 `references/`, L3 `deep/` dossiers, L4
the paper) and a personal layer in which each researcher adds their own
depth. The base package follows the field's own weights, not the owner's
research. The owner deepens his own areas (peculiar velocities, field-level
inference, Lyα tomography and voids) in his personal layer, once it can hold
that content (step B below).

## What to do, in order

### A. Generality corrections (all nine audit proposals, adopted 2026-10-20)

The owner's decision: "this package needs to be general." Content taken out
of the base for balance moves to the owner's personal layer; no verified
statement is deleted without a home. Evidence and exact locations for each
item are in the audit report, section "Proposed corrections".

1. **Rebalance the hub.** Field-state sections for BAO/full shape,
   supernovae, clusters, CMB × LSS, inflation and reionisation, or PV and Lyα
   cut to one line with a pointer; checklists and estimator conventions drawn
   from several probe families, not only Lyα P1D and the PV likelihood.
2. **Methods by method, not by code.** In PV, beyond-two-point and
   supernovae, cite each method's originators first and name flip as one
   implementation among its peers; retitle beyond-two-point `results.md` §5.4
   "Gaussian field-level likelihood".
3. **Deepen galaxy clustering and CMB** to their field weight: one deepening
   pass each, with the clustering subtopics re-sourced beyond the one paper
   that supplies five of them; consider a bispectrum dossier.
4. **Fold the two PV hub files** (`pv-surveys.md`, `fsigma8-pv-compilation.md`)
   into the standard five reference files. This lifts, for this merge, the
   rule that agents do not edit those owner-vetted files.
5. **Re-anchor the Lyα subtopics on general reviews**; let CLAMATO and
   LATIS lead the tomography dossier; keep the void–forest β at L2 in one
   skill.
6. **Shrink flip in `cosmo-software-ecosystem`** (§8.1) to the size of
   other single codes, and remove the development-copy paragraph in
   `cosmo-multimessenger`.
7. **Add the missing domains** found by the audit: alternative distance
   probes (quasar X-ray–UV, GRBs, HII galaxies, redshift drift), the
   cosmological principle, galaxy-scale strong lensing; state the out-of-
   scope topics (laboratory dark-matter searches, high-energy particles) in
   the README. Form decided 2026-10-20: sections and router lines in the
   existing `cosmo-distance-ladder-h0`, `cosmo-supernovae` and
   `cosmo-tensions-anomalies` skills; no new skill.
8. **One home each for voids and field-level inference**, with the other
   skills pointing to it.
9. **Neutral README examples**: personal-layer examples spread over several
   domains; reword "note for my Lyα work".

Cost: 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 8 and 9 are editing work (no research agent; one
gate each). 3 needs two research runs. 7 needs one research run.

### B. Phase 5 — personal layers v1

The prerequisite for the owner's own deepening. No research agent.

- Layer entries that add to, override or suppress base content by section
  key (the roadmap designed this around a claims table that was dropped; it
  is redesigned on section keys).
- `layers.py resolve`; `sync_layers.py`, which reports a layer entry whose
  base section changed since the entry was written; `promote.py` for moving
  a layer entry into the base; a release manifest of added, changed and
  retired keys.
- A `cosmoskill-refine` skill: the workflow to add a note, a paper or a
  dossier to one's layer.
- Tests on fixture layers; one answering test with an override.

Then: move the content set aside in A (the PV hub files, the owner-specific
PV and Lyα depth) into the owner's personal layer.

## Rules that hold throughout

- At most two research agents at once; two Claude accounts share the
  repository: before any run, read `git log`, the `_progress.md` files and
  the end of the gate log, and take a skill only if no one holds it.
- Nothing from memory; every number read at source; a paper seen by title
  only gets no statement.
- Agents write only inside their skill; the upkeep tools run once, at the
  end of a run; the coordinator gates every run and logs it.
- The owner commits; agents do not.
