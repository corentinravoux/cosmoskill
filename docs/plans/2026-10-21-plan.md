# cosmoskill — current plan

Written 2026-10-21. This file replaces `2026-10-20-plan.md`, whose items A
to D are done; that plan and the roadmap of 2026-10-12 are kept as history.
Every gate is recorded in `docs/history/tests/gate-log.md`.

## Aim

A general observational-cosmology skill package, useful to any researcher,
with four levels (L1 `SKILL.md`, L2 `references/`, L3 `deep/` dossiers, L4
the paper) and a personal layer in which each researcher adds their own
depth. The base follows the field's own weights, not the owner's research.

### 1b. Guidance for writing a layer

Personal layers have a format and a workflow (`cosmoskill-refine`,
`docs/layers.md`) but no advice on writing entries that work. Every answer
in a skill reads everything `layers.py <skill>` prints, so a poorly written
layer costs tokens on every question and attaches badly. No research agent;
one gate.

1. **Advice, "Writing good entries"**, in `skills/cosmoskill-refine/SKILL.md`
   (short form) and `docs/layers.md` (with reasons):
   - Size: a note is 1–3 lines (one fact, preference or correction);
     longer content goes in a dossier, read only when the question is on
     its subject; the profile holds identity and conventions, not content;
     few `general` notes per skill.
   - Attachment: the most specific section key; the note opens with its
     subject ("τ prior:", "For KiDS S8:") so that relevance shows without
     opening the section; one entry per subject, no two contradicting
     entries on one key.
   - Kind: note to add without contradicting; override when the base value
     is not the one used, with value, paper and place read; suppress with a
     reason; dossier for one's own method depth, in the package's template.
   - Sources: every number with its paper and place read; papers only
     through `layers.py source`; experience and judgement under "Not
     verified" or labelled as one's own.
   - Updates: write an override after reading the current base with
     `layers.py resolve`; never paste base text into a note.
   - Privacy and promotion: unpublished or collaboration-internal content in
     `private/` or `private: yes`; general entries written neutrally so
     that `promote.py` can take them unchanged.
2. **One example layer**, in `docs/examples/layer/`: a profile, notes of
   each kind (add, override, suppress, `general`) on two or three skills, a
   paper row and one short dossier, on a neutral subject, built with
   `layers.py` itself and passing `layers.py check` and `sync_layers.py`;
   a test keeps it valid as the base changes.
3. **`cosmoskill-refine` uses the example** when a researcher builds a
   specialised part of a skill in their layer (their own notes, papers and
   dossiers for a subject the base covers only broadly): it reads the
   example first and follows its shape, size and labelling, and applies the
   advice above to every entry it writes.
4. **`layers.py check` warns** (does not fail) on a note longer than about
   three lines and on more than a few `general` notes in one skill.

### 4. Rebalance the dossiers that lean on one group, code or dataset

About 40 dossiers or sections were flagged (gate log, the "C" sections of
2026-10-20 and 21). The rule for each:

- Read the primary papers of the other groups, codes or datasets that do
  the same thing, and give each method step its peers. Keep every verified
  statement already there; nothing is deleted without a home.
- Target: where the field has several groups, no single group, code or
  dataset supplies more than about half of the dossier's citations. Where
  the field really has one (one instrument, one survey), the dossier says so.
- Owner-specific depth that the rebalancing displaces goes to the set-aside
  record and, if the owner wants it, to his personal layer.
- At most two research agents at once; the coordinator gates each run
  (checkers, keys, tests, two statements compared at source) and logs it.

## Rules that hold throughout

- At most two research agents at once; two Claude accounts share the
  repository: before any run, read `git log`, the `_progress.md` files and
  the end of the gate log, and take a skill only if no one holds it.
- Nothing from memory; every number read at source; a paper seen by title
  only gets no statement.
- Agents write only inside their skill; the upkeep tools run once, at the
  end of a run; the coordinator gates every run and logs it.
- The owner commits; agents do not.
