# Scopes of the seven added domains

The first build left seven parts of the field without a skill. This file
fixes the scope of each and its boundary with the skills that already touch
it, so that a number has one home. Where a boundary says "stays in", the
new skill gives the essentials in two or three sentences and names the
other skill.

| Skill | Holds | Stays elsewhere |
|---|---|---|
| `cosmo-data-pipelines` | How raw data become catalogues and maps: spectroscopic pipelines (target selection, fibre assignment, extraction, sky subtraction, redshift fitting and its success rate), imaging pipelines (detrending, astrometry, PSF modelling, deblending, co-addition), CMB time-ordered data to maps, catalogue construction (masks, randoms, completeness, imaging-systematics weights), data-release practice and validation. | Facility facts and dates: `cosmo-surveys-facilities`. Photometric calibration and redshift estimation: `cosmo-photometry-photoz`. The effect of each pipeline step on a cosmological result: the probe skills. Codes as software: `cosmo-software-ecosystem`. |
| `cosmo-photometry-photoz` | Photometric calibration (global calibration, forward calibration, standards, chromatic effects, Milky Way extinction); photometric redshifts by template fitting and by machine learning; calibration of redshift distributions (direct, self-organising maps, clustering redshifts, shear ratio); redshift probability distributions and their storage; metrics, requirements and challenge results; spectroscopic training samples and their incompleteness. | The priors each lensing survey adopts on its redshift distributions and their effect on S8: `cosmo-weak-lensing`. Supernova photometric calibration: `cosmo-supernovae`. Photometric cluster redshifts: `cosmo-clusters`. |
| `cosmo-cosmic-web` | The web as an object of study: classification schemes and structure finders, filaments, walls, nodes and voids as structures, their statistics and evolution; dependence of halo and galaxy properties on web environment; tidal alignments and spin; gas in filaments and the missing baryons; protoclusters; reconstructions of the web from galaxies and from the Lyα forest. | Cosmological constraints from voids and density-split statistics: `cosmo-beyond-two-point`. Lyα tomography methods and data: `cosmo-lyman-alpha`. Constrained simulations of the local web: `cosmo-simulations-mocks`. Intrinsic alignments as a lensing systematic: `cosmo-weak-lensing`. |
| `cosmo-bbn` | Big-bang nucleosynthesis as a probe: primordial abundances of deuterium, helium-4, helium-3 and lithium-7 and how each is measured; nuclear reaction rates and their laboratory measurements; the neutron lifetime; BBN codes and their agreement; the baryon density from BBN and its use as a prior; the lithium problem; limits on N_eff and on new physics from BBN alone and with the CMB. | The synthesis of N_eff and neutrino-mass bounds: `cosmo-neutrinos-dark-matter`. How BAO analyses use the BBN prior to calibrate the sound horizon: `cosmo-galaxy-clustering` and `cosmo-distance-ladder-h0`. Helium and N_eff from the CMB damping tail: `cosmo-cmb`. |
| `cosmo-galaxy-halo-connection` | How galaxies occupy haloes, as measured: halo occupation distributions per tracer, subhalo abundance matching, conditional luminosity functions, the stellar-to-halo mass relation, empirical and semi-analytic models, assembly bias and its detection, satellite fractions and kinematics, galaxy–galaxy lensing with clustering and the "lensing is low" discrepancy; halo definitions, mass functions, bias and concentration relations; the halo model and its limits. | How mocks are populated and validated for a survey: `cosmo-simulations-mocks`. Cluster mass calibration: `cosmo-clusters`. Bias expansions in perturbation theory: `cosmo-theory-foundations`. Cosmology from small-scale clustering with emulators: `cosmo-beyond-two-point` and `cosmo-galaxy-clustering`. |
| `cosmo-early-universe-exotica` | Physics of the early Universe beyond the standard inflationary and thermal history, and the observational limits on each: primordial black holes, cosmic strings and other defects, first-order phase transitions, the stochastic gravitational-wave background and the pulsar-timing signal, early dark energy and modified recombination, primordial magnetic fields, axion-like particles, variation of fundamental constants, CMB spectral distortions, energy injection during the dark ages. | Inflationary observables (n_s, r, f_NL, isocurvature, features, birefringence): `cosmo-inflation-primordial`. Early dark energy as a resolution of the H0 tension, status only: `cosmo-tensions-anomalies`. Dark-matter particle models: `cosmo-neutrinos-dark-matter`. Standard sirens: `cosmo-multimessenger`. |
| `cosmo-machine-learning` | Machine learning as a method in cosmology, across probes: emulators of summary statistics and their error budgets, neural compression and field-level networks, neural posterior, likelihood and ratio estimation, generative models of fields and catalogues, learned corrections of fast simulations, classification of transients and spectra, anomaly detection; training sets and benchmark suites; robustness to model misspecification and to the simulation used for training; interpretability; reproducibility practice. | Validation of simulation-based inference as statistics (coverage, calibration tests): `cosmo-statistics-inference`. Cosmological results obtained with these methods: `cosmo-beyond-two-point` and the probe skills. Photometric redshifts by machine learning: `cosmo-photometry-photoz`. Codes as software: `cosmo-software-ecosystem`. |

Order of the builds: `cosmo-photometry-photoz`, `cosmo-galaxy-halo-connection`,
`cosmo-data-pipelines`, `cosmo-bbn`, `cosmo-machine-learning`,
`cosmo-cosmic-web`, `cosmo-early-universe-exotica`. The first three come
first because the older skills lean on them most.
