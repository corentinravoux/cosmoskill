# Level test — questions

Sixteen questions, each from the point of view of a researcher: an outsider
to the domain, an analyst of the same probe, an analyst of another probe, a
referee, an author about to quote a number. Expected levels are kept in
`expected.md`, which the answering session does not read.

1. (outsider) Is the S8 tension between cosmic shear and the CMB resolved?
2. (outsider) In a few sentences, where does the Lyα forest BAO measurement stand today?
3. (analyst of another probe) Does the kinematic SZ effect give a measurement of fσ8?
4. (outsider) Is CMB-S4 still going ahead?
5. (referee) I am refereeing a cluster-count cosmology paper. What should I check?
6. (outsider) What is the latest Hubble-constant value from gravitational-wave standard sirens?
7. (analyst of the same probe) Which wavenumber and redshift ranges do the DESI DR1 P1D measurements cover, for each estimator?
8. (analyst) My covariance comes from 1000 mocks and my data vector has 200 points, with 10 parameters. By how much must the error bars be widened?
9. (analyst of another probe) On which date was Euclid's Q2 release made public, and what does it contain?
10. (analyst) What does the "everything" variant of the Local Distance Network give for H0?
11. (analyst of another probe) What is the tightest 21 cm power-spectrum limit of HERA Phase II during the epoch of reionisation, and at which k and z?
12. (analyst) How many supernovae does Pantheon+ contain, and what Ω_m does it give in flat ΛCDM?
13. (analyst of the same probe) Take me through the derivation of the correction factors for a covariance estimated from mocks, and tell me how to choose the number of mocks for a 300-point data vector.
14. (author) My draft says "DESI DR2 measures D_H/r_d = 8.632 ± 0.101 from the Lyα forest". Can I submit it like that?
15. (analyst of the same probe; a personal layer exists for this question, see the instructions) Summarise what the DESI DR1 P1D says about cosmology.
16. (outsider) How significant is the Hubble tension?
