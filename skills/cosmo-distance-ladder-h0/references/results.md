# Results — current H0 measurements, by route, with the history of the key numbers

Scope: headline values of the Hubble constant from the local distance ladder,
from ladder-independent routes, and from the early-Universe / inverse-ladder
side, plus derived tension significances.
Snapshot: 2026-10-26

All H0 values are in km/s/Mpc. Flags: `[src]` read this session in the cited
paper (abstract unless a section is named); `[sec]` taken from a secondary
source that is named; `[der]` derived here, with the derivation stated.
"Model" is the cosmological model the number depends on; a ladder value
depends on cosmology only through the low-redshift expansion (q0), which is
stated where the source states it.

Megamasers now give two incompatible readings of the same Megamaser
Cosmology Project sample (six galaxies in one analysis, five in the other). With
the same family of velocity reconstruction, one group finds 71.6 ± 3.7
(arXiv:2609.17684, abstract) and the other 66.9 ± 2.5, 68.4 ± 2.5 and
68.8 ± 2.6 for velocity noise of 50, 100 and 150 km/s (arXiv:2608.06247,
Table 2); see
`open-questions.md`.

Cosmic chronometers measure H(z), not H0 directly. The newest set is H(z) =
88.48 ± 0.57 (stat) ± 12.32 (syst) at z = 0.46, 119.45 ± 6.39 ± 16.64 at
z = 0.67 and 108.28 ± 10.07 ± 15.08 at z = 0.83 from 360,000 DESI DR1 passive
galaxies `[src: abstract]` (Loubser 2025, arXiv:2511.02730). The systematic
term, set by stellar-population modelling, dominates; current accuracy is
about 5% at z ~ 0.5 `[src: abstract]` (Moresco 2024, arXiv:2412.01994).

## 5. Tension significance, as published
<!-- key: tension-significance-computed-uniformly -->

The significance depends on both endpoints; quote the one a paper states,
with its endpoints. Published values:

- 5.0 sigma for SH0ES 2022 against Planck, computed in 5 log H0 as
  0.176 ± 0.035 mag `[src: Sect. 5]` (arXiv:2112.04510).
- 5.8 sigma for the four-anchor SH0ES ladder against the CMB in ΛCDM
  `[src: abstract]` (arXiv:2404.08038).
- 7.1 sigma against Planck+SPT+ACT and 5.0 sigma against BBN + DESI DR2 BAO
  for H0DN `[src: abstract]` (arXiv:2510.23823).
- 6.2 sigma for SPT-3G alone against SH0ES `[src: abstract]` and 6.4 sigma
  for CMB-SPA against SH0ES `[src: Sect. VII.2.2]` (arXiv:2506.20707).
- 4.5 sigma between DESI + BBN and SH0ES "independent of the CMB"
  `[src: Sect. VI]` (arXiv:2503.14738).
- "~6 sigma" above the ΛCDM + CMB expectation for 73.18 ± 0.88
  `[src: abstract]` (arXiv:2509.01667).
- 5.6 sigma above Planck for the seven-route summary 73.30 ± 0.92
  `[src: abstract]` (arXiv:2606.26831).
- 3.7 sigma with Planck for a "no-SN" late-Universe combination built on
  TDCOSMO-2026 with DESI BAO for Omega_m, and 2.7 sigma with Planck after
  excluding the whole local distance ladder `[src: abstract]`
  (TDCOSMO Collaboration 2026, arXiv:2610.14881).

Significances that do not follow the Gaussian rule: a historical recalibration
that treats the scatter of 163 published values (1976 to 2019) as the true
error distribution turns a nominal 4.4 sigma into 2.1 sigma
`[src: abstract]` (Lopez-Corredoira 2024, arXiv:2411.13621); a Bayesian
jackknife over 16 measurements gives a model-averaged 95% region
66.7 < H0 < 72.7 `[src: abstract]` (Hughes et al. 2025, arXiv:2511.19341).

The central value has moved little since 2016 while the error shrank. The
2019 and 2021 values (arXiv:1903.07603, arXiv:2012.08534) are superseded; cite
73.04 (arXiv:2112.04510, Eq. 10) as the last single-team HST-only baseline,
73.17 or 73.18 (arXiv:2404.08038, arXiv:2509.01667, abstract) as the latest
SH0ES values, and 73.50 (arXiv:2510.23823, abstract) as the community
combination.

CCHP line: 69.8 ± 0.6 (stat) ± 1.6 (sys) from HST TRGB + CSP in 2021
`[src: abstract]` (Freedman 2021, arXiv:2106.15656), then 70.39 ± 1.22 ± 1.33
± 0.70 with JWST `[src: abstract]` (arXiv:2408.06153). These are the numbers of the current arXiv
abstract; earlier versions of the preprint were not checked this session, so
verify the version before quoting an older CCHP JWST value.

Time-delay cosmography `[src: abstract]`: 73.3 (+1.7/-1.8) with assumed
profiles in 2019 (arXiv:1907.04869), 74.5 (+5.6/-6.1) once the mass-sheet
degeneracy was freed in 2020 (arXiv:2007.02941), 71.6 (+3.9/-3.3) from the 8
TDCOSMO-2025 lenses with a Pantheon+ Omega_m in 2025 (arXiv:2506.03023),
**superseded** by 71.1 (+2.4/-2.3) from the 11 TDCOSMO-2026 lenses with a
DES-Dovekie Omega_m (arXiv:2610.14781). TDCOSMO-2026 itself gives the
TDCOSMO-2025 numbers it replaces: 5.0% to 3.3% for the lenses alone with SNe,
4.6% (74.3 +3.1/-3.7) to 3.0% (71.3 +2.2/-2.1) with SLACS + SL2S
`[src: Sect. VIII.1]` (arXiv:2610.14781). The 2.4% H0LiCOW number
(arXiv:1907.04869, abstract) is superseded as a precision claim, not as a
central value.

Standard sirens (LVK): 75.4 (+12.8/-9.1) from 142 GWTC-4.0 sources with
GLADE+ and GW170817 (LVK 2025, arXiv:2509.04348, abstract), **superseded** by
71.7 (+9.4/-7.5) from 236 GWTC-5.0 sources with DES-Y6 and GW170817 (LVK 2026,
arXiv:2605.27227, abstract).

CMB: 67.4 ± 0.5 (Planck 2018, arXiv:1807.06209, abstract) to 67.19 ± 0.38
(SPT + ACT + Planck 2025, arXiv:2506.20707, abstract). The ground-based SPT-3G
value alone, 66.66 ± 0.60 (same abstract), is lower than Planck's.
