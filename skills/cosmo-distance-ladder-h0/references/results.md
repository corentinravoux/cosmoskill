# Results — current H0 measurements, by route, with the history of the key numbers

Scope: headline values of the Hubble constant from the local distance ladder,
from ladder-independent routes, and from the early-Universe / inverse-ladder
side, plus derived tension significances.
Snapshot: 2026-10-19

All H0 values are in km/s/Mpc. Flags: `[src]` read this session in the cited
paper (abstract unless a section is named); `[sec]` taken from a secondary
source that is named; `[der]` derived here, with the derivation stated.
"Model" is the cosmological model the number depends on; a ladder value
depends on cosmology only through the low-redshift expansion (q0), which is
stated where the source states it.

Megamasers now give two incompatible readings of the same Megamaser
Cosmology Project sample (six galaxies in one analysis, five in the other). With
the same family of velocity reconstruction, one group finds 71.6 ± 3.7 and the
other 66.9 to 68.8 ± 2.5; see `open-questions.md`.

Cosmic chronometers measure H(z), not H0 directly. The newest set is H(z) =
88.48 ± 0.57 (stat) ± 12.32 (syst) at z = 0.46, 119.45 ± 6.39 ± 16.64 at
z = 0.67 and 108.28 ± 10.07 ± 15.08 at z = 0.83 from 360,000 DESI DR1 passive
galaxies `[src]` (Loubser 2025, arXiv:2511.02730). The systematic term, set by
stellar-population modelling, dominates; current accuracy is about 5% at
z ~ 0.5 `[src]` (Moresco 2024, arXiv:2412.01994).

## 5. Tension significance, computed uniformly
<!-- key: tension-significance-computed-uniformly -->

Derivation `[der]`: n = (H0_local - H0_early) / sqrt(sigma_local^2 +
sigma_early^2), assuming Gaussian, uncorrelated errors in H0. Asymmetric
errors were symmetrised; the CCHP error is the quadrature total 1.94.

| Local value | vs Planck 2018 (67.4 ± 0.5) | vs SPT-3G D1 (66.66 ± 0.60) | vs CMB-SPA (67.19 ± 0.38) | vs DESI DR2 + BBN (68.51 ± 0.58) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SH0ES 2022, 73.04 ± 1.04 | 4.9 | 5.3 | 5.3 | 3.8 |
| SH0ES 2024, 73.17 ± 0.86 | 5.8 | 6.2 | 6.4 | 4.5 |
| SH0ES 2025, 73.18 ± 0.88 | 5.7 | 6.1 | 6.2 | 4.4 |
| H0DN 2025, 73.50 ± 0.81 | 6.4 | 6.8 | 7.1 | 5.0 |
| CCHP TRGB, 70.39 ± 1.94 | 1.5 | 1.8 | 1.6 | 0.9 |
| TDCOSMO-2025, 71.6 ± 3.6 | 1.2 | 1.4 | 1.2 | 0.8 |
| MCP 2020, 73.9 ± 3.0 | 2.1 | 2.4 | 2.2 | 1.8 |
| SBF 2025, 73.8 ± 2.4 | 2.6 | 2.9 | 2.7 | 2.1 |

Published significances that these reproduce: 5.0 sigma for SH0ES 2022 against
Planck, computed in 5 log H0 as 0.176 ± 0.035 mag `[src]` (arXiv:2112.04510,
Sec. 5); 5.8 sigma for the four-anchor ladder `[src]` (arXiv:2404.08038);
7.1 sigma against Planck+SPT+ACT and 5.0 sigma against BBN + DESI DR2 BAO for
H0DN `[src]` (arXiv:2510.23823); 6.2 sigma for SPT-3G alone against SH0ES
`[src]` and 6.4 sigma for CMB-SPA against SH0ES `[src]` (arXiv:2506.20707,
abstract and body); 4.5 sigma between DESI + BBN and SH0ES "independent of the
CMB" `[src]` (arXiv:2503.14738, Sec. VI); "~6 sigma" in arXiv:2509.01667
`[src]`.

Significances that do not follow the Gaussian rule: a historical recalibration
that treats the scatter of 163 published values (1976 to 2019) as the true
error distribution turns a nominal 4.4 sigma into 2.1 sigma `[src]`
(Lopez-Corredoira 2024, arXiv:2411.13621); a Bayesian jackknife over 16
measurements gives a model-averaged 95% region 66.7 < H0 < 72.7 `[src]`
(Hughes et al. 2025, arXiv:2511.19341).

The central value has stayed within 73.0 to 74.0 since 2016 while the error
halved. Values of 74.03 and 73.2 are superseded; cite 73.04 (2022) as the last
single-team HST-only baseline, 73.17 or 73.18 as the latest SH0ES values, and
73.50 as the community combination.

CCHP line: 69.8 ± 0.6 (stat) ± 1.6 (sys) from HST TRGB + CSP in 2021 `[src]`
(Freedman 2021, arXiv:2106.15656), then 70.39 ± 1.22 ± 1.33 ± 0.70 with JWST
`[src]` (arXiv:2408.06153). These are the numbers of the current arXiv
abstract; earlier versions of the preprint were not checked this session, so
verify the version before quoting an older CCHP JWST value.

Time-delay cosmography: 73.3 (+1.7/-1.8) with assumed profiles in 2019
(arXiv:1907.04869), 74.5 (+5.6/-6.1) once the mass-sheet degeneracy was freed
in 2020 (arXiv:2007.02941), 71.6 (+3.9/-3.3) in 2025 (arXiv:2506.03023). The
2.4% H0LiCOW number is superseded as a precision claim, not as a central
value.

CMB: 67.4 ± 0.5 (Planck 2018) to 67.19 ± 0.38 (SPT + ACT + Planck 2025). The
ground-based SPT-3G value alone, 66.66 ± 0.60, is lower than Planck's.
