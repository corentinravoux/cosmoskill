# Surveys and data — the programmes and releases behind every H0 number

Scope: instruments, samples, anchors, release dates and status of the data
sets used to measure the Hubble constant locally, through one-step routes, and
through the early Universe; past, current and planned, in that order.
Snapshot: 2026-10-07

Flags: `[src]` read this session in the cited paper or on the official page
named; `[sec]` from a named secondary source; `[der]` derived here.
Sample sizes are those of the cited analysis, not of the parent survey.

Sample-size facts that decide arguments:

- The HST ladder has 42 calibrator SNe Ia and four anchors; the first JWST
  subsamples had about a quarter of that with one anchor `[src]`
  (arXiv:2408.11770).
- The JWST Cepheid set now covers more than 50% of the SH0ES calibrators
  `[src]` (arXiv:2509.01667).
- 11 of the 35 TRGB hosts were not in the CCHP compilation of 24, and those 11
  alone give 74.1 `[src: abstract]` (arXiv:2504.08921).

## 9. The community products
<!-- key: the-community-products -->

- **H0 Distance Network (H0DN).** A collaboration formed at an ISSI Bern
  workshop; members reviewed the data sets and voted indicators into a
  baseline; open-source software and data products accompany the paper
  `[src]` (arXiv:2510.23823). First report October 2025. Follow-ups already
  plug new inputs into it: a Gaia-only TRGB calibration (arXiv:2602.15421)
  and corrected SN samples (arXiv:2607.24443).
- **Reviews to start from.** Chen & Wang 2026 (arXiv:2606.26831) for the
  ladder; Cai & Wang 2026 (arXiv:2606.20434) for the tension and its
  theoretical side; Verde et al. 2023 (arXiv:2311.13305) for the taxonomy of
  H0's; Freedman & Madore 2023 (arXiv:2309.05618) for the CCHP view; the
  CosmoVerse white paper (Di Valentino et al. 2025, arXiv:2504.01669) for the
  community-wide programme.

The five-year outlook of the 2026 ladder review: a reliable 1% local H0 needs
larger calibrator samples, cross-validated level-1 zero points, explicit
covariance propagation and pre-specified selection criteria `[src]`
(arXiv:2606.26831).
