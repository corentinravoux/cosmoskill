Scope: the galaxy samples, group catalogues, lensing source catalogues and
simulation suites on which galaxy–halo measurements are made, with what each
contributed, in the order past, current, next.

## 3. Group and cluster catalogues
<!-- key: group-and-cluster-catalogues -->

- SDSS DR4 halo-based groups: 301 237 groups, two halo-mass estimates per group
  (luminosity, stellar mass), public (Yang et al. 2007, arXiv:0707.4640) [src].
  Basis of the conformity (arXiv:astro-ph/0509147) and secondary-bias
  (arXiv:2511.19607) results [src].
- DESI DR1 BGS self-calibrated groups: calibrated on the clustering of the
  sample itself; a Hybrid Nearest Neighbour scheme assigns redshifts to
  fibre-incomplete targets, correct about 50% of the time, with unbiased
  occupation statistics (arXiv:2610.20919) [src]. Haloes are M_200m with NFW
  profiles on a mean c(M) relation [src: Sect. IV.1, Eqs. 2–3]; halo masses
  carry a typical ~0.2 dex error, larger for galaxies without satellites and
  for "lost" centrals, and must be checked on mocks before use [src: Sect.
  VI.2].
- SDSS redMaPPer clusters, 8648 at 0.1 < z < 0.33: the halo assembly-bias claim
  and its critiques (arXiv:1506.06135, arXiv:1611.00366) [src]. Cluster
  catalogues as cosmological probes are in `cosmo-clusters`.

## 6. Which measurement constrains which part of the connection
<!-- key: which-measurement-constrains-which-part-of-the -->

The review groups the measurements into galaxy abundance, two-point
clustering, group and cluster catalogues, weak lensing and additional
observables [src: Sect. 3 of arXiv:1804.03097, section titles]. What each
data type pins down, in the analyses read:

| Measurement | Constrains | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Stellar mass or luminosity function | the mean SHMR via abundance matching; not the scatter | arXiv:0903.4682, arXiv:1001.0015 [src]; [src: Sect. 6.2 of arXiv:1804.03097] |
| Projected clustering w_p | bias, hence halo mass and scatter where bias varies steeply (log M_h ≳ 12.7); satellite fraction via the one-halo term | arXiv:1005.2413 [src]; [src: Sect. 6.2 of arXiv:1804.03097] |
| Redshift-space clustering ξ(r_p, π) | velocity bias and assembly-bias terms | arXiv:1407.4811, arXiv:2010.04182 [src] |
| Group catalogues | central scatter at fixed group mass; non-parametric HODs; quenched fractions | arXiv:1207.2160, arXiv:2610.20919 [src] |
| Galaxy–galaxy lensing ΔΣ | mean halo mass directly; with w_p, the matter clustering amplitude | arXiv:1104.0928, arXiv:2111.02419 [src] |
| Satellite kinematics | halo mass and its scatter at low mass; orbital anisotropy; insensitive to assembly bias by construction | arXiv:1003.3203, arXiv:2409.03105 [src] |
| Satellite counts N_sat around centrals | subtraction of satellite contributions to w_p and ΔΣ | arXiv:2609.04351 [src] |
| Excess counts of photometric objects around spectroscopic tracers (PAC) | SHMR to dwarf masses beyond the spectroscopic limit | arXiv:2603.29331 [src] |
| Group–galaxy cross-correlation + lensing vs large-scale density | direct HOD as a function of environment (assembly bias) | arXiv:2510.20896 [src] |
| Higher-order statistics (void probability, group multiplicity) | parts of P(N\|M) that w_p does not fix | arXiv:astro-ph/0109001, arXiv:1708.04892 [src] |

## 8. What comes next
<!-- key: what-comes-next -->

- DESI: DR1 is the latest release used in the HOD, SHMR, group, assembly-bias
  and lensing papers read; the first DR2 (Y3) galaxy–halo test is LRG
  multiplet clustering, which the One-Percent HOD mocks fail
  (arXiv:2511.15354) [src: Sect. 6.1]. The papers read do not state a date for the DR2
  galaxy–halo analyses; see `cosmo-surveys-facilities` for release dates.
- Lensing overlap: UNIONS (~2650 deg² with CMASS) is the largest overlap quoted
  here (arXiv:2606.23651) [src]; Euclid and Rubin LSST overlaps with DESI are
  expected to dominate later, with their schedules in
  `cosmo-surveys-facilities`.
- Stage-V spectroscopy: the DESI assembly-bias paper presents its method as one
  for "DESI and future Stage-V surveys" (arXiv:2510.20896) [src].
