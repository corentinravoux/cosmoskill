Consequences stated by the DESI DR2 AP paper (arXiv:2607.27410) [src]: the Lyα AP Ω_m is 1.4σ higher than the DESI BAO value; adding it moves the DESI-versus-CMB discrepancy in ΛCDM from 2.4σ to 2.2σ; with the joint DESI DR2 BAO + Lyα full-shape data, w0waCDM is preferred over ΛCDM at 2.7σ (DESI + CMB) and 3.2σ (with supernovae). For comparison, the DR2 BAO key paper quotes 3.1σ for DESI BAO + CMB and 2.8–4.2σ with supernovae depending on the sample (arXiv:2503.14738) [src]. The dark-energy interpretation belongs to `cosmo-dark-energy-modified-gravity`.

### 1.0b The DESI DR2 full-shape fit in detail (paper body)
<!-- key: the-desi-dr2-full-shape-fit-in-detail-paper-body -->

All from arXiv:2607.27410, Sects. 3 and 5 [src].

- Parameters: φ ≡ q_⊥/q_∥ = [D_M/D_H]/[D_M/D_H]_fid and α ≡ sqrt(q_⊥ q_∥), with separate pairs for the BAO peak (φ_p, α_p) and for the smooth component (φ_s, α_s); α_s is marginalised as a nuisance parameter. The fiducial values are those of the BAO analysis (Planck 2018; fσ8(2.33) = 0.2979).
- Fit: 11,790 data points, 402 analytically marginalised small-scale amplitudes, 21 sampled parameters; χ² = 11492.0 for 11388 − 21, PTE = 0.20. Scale cut r_min = 40 h⁻¹Mpc for the cross-correlation (30 in the BAO analysis). Vega + PolyChord, iminuit for the best fit.
- φ_s and φ_p are nearly independent (correlation below 1%) and agree, which is used as a consistency check.
- BAO systematic term, revised relative to the BAO paper: Δα_p = 0.15% and Δφ_p = 0.8%, uncorrelated, because both AbacusSummit and CoLoRe-2LPT mocks show a larger effect in the AP direction of the peak. No systematic term is added to φ_s. The shift is "at most … about 1/3 of our current statistical uncertainty in BAO", and no correction is applied.
- Final distances correspond to 0.84% on D_M/r_d and 0.77% on D_H/r_d, a 6.7-fold reduction of the contour area relative to DESI DR1 BAO, and exceed the original DESI 5-year design goal of about 0.9% on each from BAO alone.
- Position relative to models: "in excellent agreement with the ΛCDM prediction" from Planck, and "a roughly 2σ tension with the w0waCDM prediction" from CMB + DESI BAO.

Conversions for the DR1 row, derived here with c = 299 792.458 km/s and the paper's 147.09 Mpc normalisation: D_H/r_d = c/(239.2 × 147.09) = 8.52, with the stated 2% giving ±0.17; D_M/r_d = 5840/147.09 = 39.70, with the stated 2.4% giving ±0.95 [der].

What the history shows.

- The BOSS DR11 "2.5σ tension" (D_A low by 7%, D_H high by 7% relative to Planck ΛCDM, arXiv:1404.1801 [src]) shrank monotonically: 2.3σ at DR12, 1.7σ at DR14, 1.5σ at DR16 (citations in the table). A text that still cites a Lyα BAO tension with ΛCDM is quoting a 2015–2017 state of the data.
- D_M/r_d moved up from 35.7–37.5 (SDSS, 2017–2020) to 38.99 (DESI DR2). D_H/r_d moved down from about 9.0 to 8.63. Both DESI DR2 errors are about half the eBOSS DR16 ones [der: 0.098/0.19, 0.52/1.1].
- DESI DR2 is the first Lyα BAO analysis to carry a theoretical systematic term, motivated by studies of the BAO shift from non-linear growth (arXiv:2503.14739 [src]; the shift studies are arXiv:2412.06892 and arXiv:2503.13442).
- The eBOSS DR16 errors are statistical only (arXiv:2007.08995 [src]).

DESI DR1 P1D in numbers, as summarised in the cosmology paper (arXiv:2601.21432, Sect. 2.1) [src]: the DR1 sample has more than 450,000 quasars at z > 2.1; P1D is measured from z = 2.2 to 4.2 in steps of Δz = 0.2 (11 bins [der]); the QMLE and FFT data vectors are on Zenodo (records 16943723 and 17100543); the cosmological analysis uses the QMLE measurement restricted to forests with mean S/N per pixel > 3 (the fiducial measurement uses S/N > 1), which leaves 62,807 quasars, raises the statistical errors by about 25% and lowers the systematic errors by a factor 2.7; the k range used is 10⁻³ s/km < k_∥ < 0.5π/R_z with R_z = c Δλ / [(1+z) λ_α] and Δλ = 0.8 Å, the lower limit set by continuum-fitting errors and the upper by spectrograph resolution; the side band for metal subtraction is 1268–1380 Å; only the QMLE estimates correlations between redshift bins. DESI spectra cover 3600–9800 Å at resolution 2000–5000.

The upper limit in numbers, derived here from that formula with c = 299 792.458 km/s and λ_α = 1215.67 Å: R_z = 197.3/(1+z) km/s, so k_max = 0.5π/R_z = 0.0255 s/km at z = 2.2, 0.0318 at z = 3.0 and 0.0414 at z = 4.2 [der].

DESI DR1 status: the FFT and QMLE measurements are stated to be consistent with each other and "the most precise one-dimensional power spectrum measurement to date" (arXiv:2505.09493) [src]. The estimator-validation paper used 20 synthetic 1D realisations of DR1 and CCD image simulations of 675,000 quasars; the image simulations show a resolution error of a few percent at the highest k, which sets the DR1 resolution systematic budget and is not applied as a correction (arXiv:2509.13593) [src].

Wavenumber ranges of the four DESI measurement papers, each in the unit its paper uses [src, sections named]:

- EDR FFT (arXiv:2306.06311, Sect. 7): 0.145 ≤ k ≤ 2 Å⁻¹ in 9 redshift bins from 2.2 to 3.8; k_max = 2.0 Å⁻¹ is reached "conservatively" for all redshift bins, against 1.54 Å⁻¹ (z = 2.2) and 1.03 Å⁻¹ (z = 3.8) for eBOSS. The paper works in Å⁻¹ and converts with k[s/km] = k[Å⁻¹] × λ_α (1+z)/c.
- EDR QMLE (arXiv:2306.06316, Sects. 3.2.3 and 5): velocity units; 20 linear bins of 0.5 × 10⁻³ s/km and 13 logarithmic bins of Δlog k = 0.05; Δz = 0.2 from z = 2.0 to 3.8 included; observed wavelengths 3600–6000 Å. Band edges: 20 × 0.0005 = 0.010 s/km, then 0.010 × 10^(13 × 0.05) = 0.045 s/km [der, assuming the linear bins start at k = 0 and a base-10 logarithm; the paper states the binning, not the end points].
- DR1 FFT (arXiv:2505.09493, Sects. 3.2, 4.1 and 7): Å⁻¹; k_min = 2π/(L_sub (1+z_min)) = 0.0468 Å⁻¹ with sub-forests of L_sub = 43.3 Å in the rest frame and z_min = 2.1; k_max = 2.0 Å⁻¹, "the same maximal wavenumber value" as in EDR, where the resolution correction is a factor 5; no P1D above z = 4.2. The cross-exposure estimator is used at z < 2.7 and the noise-subtracted one at z > 2.7.
- DR1 QMLE (arXiv:2505.07974, Sect. 4.1): velocity units; 60 linear bins of 5 × 10⁻⁴ s/km and 25 logarithmic bins of Δlog k = 0.01; Δz = 0.2 from z = 2.0 to 4.6 with the first and last bins removed; observed wavelengths 3600–7000 Å; 314,241 quasars in the Lyα region. Band edges: 60 × 0.0005 = 0.030 s/km, then 0.030 × 10^(25 × 0.01) = 0.053 s/km [der, same assumptions]. The paper recommends k > 10⁻³ s/km (continuum errors) and k < 0.5π/R_z (resolution), the cuts adopted by the cosmology paper.

The two k_max conventions agree: 2.0 Å⁻¹ is 0.0260 s/km at z = 2.2 and 0.0422 s/km at z = 4.2 [der: 2.0 × 1215.67 × (1+z)/299 792.458], against 0.0255 and 0.0414 s/km for 0.5π/R_z, which is 0.5π/0.8 Å = 1.96 Å⁻¹ [der]. The difference between the upper redshift of the QMLE measurement paper (bins to 4.4 [der]) and the 4.2 quoted by the cosmology paper was not resolved here.
