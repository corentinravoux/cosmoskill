# Surveys and facilities — open questions, schedule risks and stale claims

Scope: schedule risks with the evidence for each slip, cancelled or descoped projects, the points where sibling skills differ and how each was settled at source, and the stale facility claims that still circulate.
Snapshot: 2026-10-14 (web pages fetched 2026-10-09 unless another date is given)

Flags as in `surveys-and-data.md`: `[src]`, `[sec: GC|WL|CMB|SN|CL]` (carried
over from the named sibling skill), `[doc: <URL>, <date>]` (read on that
official page on that date), `[der]`. Every future date in this file
is an announcement; label it **forecast** when it enters a text.

### 1.2 Dates that carry cosmology in the next three years
<!-- key: dates-that-carry-cosmology-in-the-next-three -->

- **Euclid DR1.** DR1-Foundation on 12 November 2026 and the full DR1, with
  the clustering and lensing products, in mid-2027; the ESA page calls all
  release dates "tentative" [doc: https://www.cosmos.esa.int/web/euclid/timeline, 2026-10-26]. The split was announced on the ESA DR1
  page updated 15 June 2026 [doc: https://www.cosmos.esa.int/web/euclid/dr1-timeline, 2026-10-26]. A text that gives a single Euclid DR1
  date in 2026 refers to a plan that no longer exists in that form.
- **DESI final results in 2027** and observations through 2028 [doc: https://www.desi.lbl.gov/2026/04/15/desi-reaches-mapping-milestone-surpassing-expectations/, 2026-10-26].
  The open point is the public release of DR2, which gates every external
  reanalysis of the three-year sample.
- **Rubin.** The full DP2 is "expected Nov-Dec 2026" (dp2.lsst.io) [doc: https://dp2.lsst.io/, 2026-10-26].
  DR1 is "based on the first year of LSST observations" [doc: https://rtn-011.lsst.io/, 2026-10-26];
  no date was read.
- **Roman.** In science commissioning since 22 October 2026 [doc: https://science.nasa.gov/blogs/roman/, 2026-10-26];
  first images "by early 2027" [doc: https://www.nasa.gov/news-release/nasas-dark-universe-seeking-nancy-grace-roman-space-telescope-launches/, 2026-10-26]. The survey start and first data release dates were not read.
- **4MOST, WEAVE, MOONS.** Three new multi-object spectrographs reached the
  sky between 2022 and 2026 and none had a confirmed start of its main
  survey on the pages read. Their first cosmology (4MOST CRS, WEAVE-QSO)
  should not be dated in a text without a fresh check.
- **Simons Observatory.** The project front page mentions no public data
  release [doc: https://simonsobservatory.org/, 2026-10-26] and no
  cosmological result was found [sec: CMB]; six small-aperture telescopes by 2027 and the
  large-aperture upgrade by 2028 are plans [sec: CMB].
- **LVK.** A six-month run, IR1, is "targeting a mid-January 2027 start",
  and "Plans and timeline for the fifth observing run (O5) are in
  discussion" [doc: https://www.aei.mpg.de/gwastronomy, 2026-10-26].
- **ZTF.** The NSF added USD 1.6 million for operations in 2025 and 2026,
  announced on 3 October 2024 [doc: https://www.ztf.caltech.edu/new/nsf-funds-ztf-to-work-with-vera-rubin.html, 2026-10-26];
  the ZTF news page lists no funding for later years [doc: https://www.ztf.caltech.edu/ztf-news.html, 2026-10-26]. The cosmology-grade
  reprocessing of its SN Ia sample is still awaited [sec: SN].
- **Einstein Telescope.** The site decision by the Board of Governmental
  Representatives "is expected by 2027", between Sos Enattos (Sardinia),
  the Meuse-Rhine Euroregion and Lusatia (Saxony); on 17 July Italian and
  German research organisations signed a Memorandum of Agreement backing a
  two-site (2L) Sos Enattos–Lusatia proposal (INFN page of 1 September 2026)
  [doc: https://www.infn.it/en/towards-einstein-telescope/, 2026-10-26]; the geometry (triangle
  or two L-shaped detectors) is open (arXiv:2503.12263) [src].

## 2. Cancelled, descoped or redefined projects

| Project | What changed | Consequence for a text | Flag |
|---|---|---|---|
| CMB-S4 | DOE and NSF withdrew support on 9 July 2025; orderly shutdown | every "CMB-S4 will measure" is void; the South Pole programme (SPT-3G+, BICEP Array) and the enlarged Simons Observatory are the actual successors | [sec: CMB] |
| LiteBIRD | reformation 2024–2026: one telescope instead of three, 12 bands [src: Sect. 2.3, 5], total uncertainty δr < 0.002 (68% C.L.) | MSE | the 2018 design (11.25 m aperture, 4332 spectra per exposure; Hill et al. 2018, arXiv:1810.08695 [src: abstract]) was replaced by an 11.5 m quad-mirror concept with 18,000+ fibres [src: abstract], "proposed to replace the Canada-France-Hawai'i Telescope in the 2040s" (arXiv:2503.15553) [src]; "After the December 2024 SAC meeting, the Board issued a statement that it is pausing the MSE project" (CFHT SAC report, May 2025) [doc: https://www.cfht.hawaii.edu/en/science/SAC/reports/SAC_report_May25.php, 2026-10-26] | not a facility of the 2030s; not funded as far as read | as given |

| HSC-SSP | Deep and UltraDeep layers reached about 80% of the planned integration and "are considered done" (arXiv:2108.13045) | depth of the deep layers is below the 2018 design | [src: abstract] |
| Roman spectroscopic survey | the 2021 reference survey of 2000 deg² (arXiv:2110.01829) [sec: GC] was superseded by the community-defined High Latitude Wide Area Survey: more than 5,000 square degrees in total, of which about 2,500 deg² with spectroscopy (NASA HLWAS page) [doc: https://science.nasa.gov/mission/roman-space-telescope/high-latitude-wide-area-survey/, 2026-10-26] | forecasts based on the 2000 deg² HLSS are for a different survey | as given |
| DESI | extended, not descoped: more than 47 million galaxies and quasars against the 34 million planned, footprint from 14,000 to 17,000 deg² through 2028 | the "14,000 deg², 35–40 million" description is the 2016–2022 design | [doc: https://www.desi.lbl.gov/2026/04/15/desi-reaches-mapping-milestone-surpassing-expectations/, 2026-10-26]; design numbers [sec: GC] |
| Legacy Surveys | grew from the 14,000 deg² design (arXiv:1804.08657) [src: abstract] to about 22,700 deg² jointly covered in DR11, with an i band in the south (`legacysurvey.org` DR11 page) | the footprint used for DESI targeting (DR9) is a subset | as given |

Not every redefinition is a loss, but each makes older forecasts refer to a
survey that will not exist. A forecast is tied to the design document it
was computed for; cite that document next to the number.

## 4. Stale claims that still circulate
<!-- key: stale-claims-that-still-circulate -->

Each line gives the claim, then the correction and where it was read.

1. Any future-tense statement about CMB-S4 measurements. The project lost
   agency support on 9 July 2025 [sec: CMB].
2. "LiteBIRD launches in the late 2020s and targets δr < 0.001." Target
   launch Japanese fiscal year 2036 [src: Table 2], total-uncertainty
   requirement δr < 0.002 [src: abstract] (arXiv:2607.25285).
3. "DESI will measure 34 (or 40) million redshifts over 14,000 deg² in five
   years." Done ahead of schedule with more than 47 million, extended to
   about 17,000 deg² through 2028 [sec: GC]. The 40 million figure is the
   2022 road map's (arXiv:2209.03585) [src].
4. "DESI DR2 is public." Only the BAO likelihoods and chains are; DR1 is
   the latest public spectroscopic release [src].
5. "Euclid DR1 will be released in 2026." Only DR1-Foundation (12 November
   2026, tentative); the cosmology products come in mid-2027 [doc: https://www.cosmos.esa.int/web/euclid/timeline, 2026-10-26]. Q1 and
   Q2 are not cosmology releases [sec: GC].
6. "LSST will begin survey operations by 2022" (the design paper). The
   start of the survey was announced on
   30 June 2026 [doc: https://rubinobservatory.org/news/action-rubin-lsst-begins, 2026-10-26]. "LSST DR1 after six months": DR1 is now based on
   the first year [doc: https://rtn-011.lsst.io/, 2026-10-26].
7. "WFIRST/Roman launches in 2025" (the 2019 summary). Launched 30 August
   2026 [doc: https://www.nasa.gov/news-release/nasas-dark-universe-seeking-nancy-grace-roman-space-telescope-launches/, 2026-10-26]. "The Roman HLSS covers 2000 deg²": superseded (Sect. 2).
8. "SDSS-V continues the SDSS cosmology programme." It has no galaxy
   clustering survey; the last SDSS large-scale-structure sample is eBOSS
   in DR16 (arXiv:2507.06989; arXiv:2112.02026) [src]. "DR17 is the latest
   SDSS release": DR20, 30 July 2026 [doc: https://www.sdss.org/science/publications/data-release-publications/, 2026-10-26].
9. "4MOST is in survey operations." First light was in October
   2025 and the main survey had not started on 15 June 2026 [sec: GC],
   [src]. "4MOST has about 2400 fibres" is right: 1624 low-resolution and
   812 high-resolution fibres (arXiv:1903.02464) [src: abstract], 2436
   spectra per pointing (arXiv:2606.27882) [src: abstract].
10. "WEAVE has been surveying since 2023." Only with the large IFU; the
    multi-object surveys were still expected in April 2026 [src].
11. "eROSITA's eight-pass survey" as the basis of a cluster sample. The
    public catalogues stop at three passes and cluster cosmology uses
    eRASS1 [src], [sec: CL].
12. "The ZTF SN Ia DR2 sample of 3628 supernovae constrains dark energy."
    The 3628 is the DR2 count (arXiv:2409.04346) [src: abstract]; its
    overview says the photometry is not yet suited to cosmological
    inference [sec: SN].
13. "MSE is an 11.25 m facility with 4332 fibres" (the 2018 book,
    arXiv:1810.08695 [src: abstract]). See Sect. 2.
14. "CSST launches in 2023" (or 2025, 2026). The 2025 overview says
    "scheduled to be launched in 2027" (arXiv:2507.04618) [src].
15. "KiDS covers 1500 deg²." 1347 deg² (arXiv:2503.19439) [src: abstract].
16. "The Legacy Surveys cover 14,000 deg² in grz." That is the original
    design (arXiv:1804.08657) [src: abstract]; DR11 covers about 22,700 deg²
    and adds i in the south (`legacysurvey.org` DR11 page).
17. "The Simons Observatory has three small telescopes and forecasts
    σ(r) = 0.003." The SAT complement is to double "from three to six
    telescopes" by 2027, with a **forecast** σ_r = 1.2 × 10⁻³
    (arXiv:2512.15833) [src: abstract].
18. "O4 is under way and GWTC-3 (90 events) is the current catalogue." O4
    "continued until 18 November 2025" [doc: https://www.aei.mpg.de/gwastronomy, 2026-10-26]; GWTC-4.0 has 218 candidates
    including the 90 of GWTC-3.0 (arXiv:2508.18082) [src: abstract] and the
    GWTC-5.0 population analysis 267 mergers (arXiv:2605.27226) [src:
    abstract].
19. "DSA-2000 first light in 2026" and "SKA science in 2026" come from
    older documents; SKAO's Timeline to Science page (last modified on 13
    August 2026) gives no calendar date in its text and places the start of
    science verification "Towards the end of" AA2 [doc: https://www.skao.int/en/science-users/timeline-science, 2026-10-26].
20. "Planck 2018 is the final Planck release." PR4 (2020) and the
    likelihoods built on it are current [sec: CMB].

## 5. Questions the next data will decide
<!-- key: questions-the-next-data-will-decide -->

- Whether DESI's completed survey and extension confirm the DR2 preference
  for evolving dark energy (2027): `cosmo-dark-energy-modified-gravity`.
- Whether Euclid's slitless redshifts reach the purity assumed in its
  forecasts (DR1, mid-2027): `cosmo-galaxy-clustering`.
- Whether Rubin's delivered image quality and cadence match the simulated
  strategies on which the dark-energy forecasts rest (DP2 and DR1):
  `cosmo-weak-lensing`, `cosmo-supernovae`.
- Whether the South Pole and Atacama programmes reach σ(r) ≈ 10⁻³ without
  CMB-S4 (**forecast** target, 2030s; the SO forecast is σ_r = 1.2 × 10⁻³,
  arXiv:2512.15833 [src: abstract]): `cosmo-inflation-primordial`.
- Whether 21 cm intensity mapping moves from cross-correlation detections to
  a BAO measurement (CHIME, CHORD from 2028, HIRAX, MeerKLASS, SKA-Mid):
  `cosmo-reionization-21cm`.
- Which Stage-V spectroscopic facility is built: MUST reports construction
  under way (arXiv:2605.10102) [src]; Spec-S5, WST and MSE are designs or
  proposals [sec: GC], [src].

  started; the Roman survey start; Rubin DR1 date; DESI DR2 release date.

  arXiv:2010.03477 and arXiv:2607.27772 read) and the status of the

- ZTF funding after 2026; the O5 start date; LIGO-India.
- The current SKAO schedule: the SKAO scientific-timeline page refused
  access on 2026-10-26, and the dated AA2, AA* and readiness-review

- The intermediate 4MOST forecasts of the start of operations, the earlier

  an official page on 2026-10-26 and were removed.
