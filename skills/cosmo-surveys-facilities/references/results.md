# Surveys and facilities — dated milestones

Scope: chronology of data releases and facility milestones, a table of what is public today, and the calendar of announced milestones.
Snapshot: 2026-10-07 (web pages fetched 2026-10-09 unless another date is given)

Flags as in `surveys-and-data.md`: `[doc: <URL>, <date>]` re-read on that
official page on that date; `[src]` read on the cited paper; `[sec: GC|WL|CMB|SN|CL]` carried over from the named
sibling skill, which read it at source; `[der]` derived here. A date written as a month
is the month of the arXiv posting of the release paper, taken from the
arXiv record [src], unless a page is named. This file has no cosmological
parameter values: those are in the probe skills.

## 4. How to read these tables
<!-- key: how-to-read-these-tables -->

- A release date is the day the archive opened, not the date of the first
  cosmology paper. DESI published DR1 BAO in April 2024 and opened DR1 on
  19 March 2025 [doc: https://data.desi.lbl.gov/doc/releases/, 2026-10-26]; it published DR2 BAO in March 2025 and the DR2 spectra are
  still closed (no DR2 on the release page). Euclid has had three public releases (ERO, Q1, Q2) and no
  cosmology product [doc: https://www.cosmos.esa.int/web/euclid/timeline, 2026-10-26].
- "Survey complete" and "data public" are separated by a year or more: DESI
  finished its planned survey in April 2026 with DR1 (May 2021 through
  June 2022) as the newest public spectra [doc: https://data.desi.lbl.gov/doc/releases/, 2026-10-26]; [doc: https://www.desi.lbl.gov/2026/04/15/desi-reaches-mapping-milestone-surpassing-expectations/, 2026-10-26].
- Several numbers describe designs, not delivered data. eROSITA planned eight
  all-sky passes (arXiv:2010.03477) [src: abstract] and its latest release
  covers three (arXiv:2607.27772). The Legacy Surveys were designed for
  14,000 deg² (arXiv:1804.08657) [src: abstract] and DR11 covers
  22,731 deg² jointly in all bands with at least one pass [doc: https://www.legacysurvey.org/dr11/description/, 2026-10-26]. KiDS was
  designed for 1500 deg² (arXiv:1206.1254) [src: abstract] and DR5 spans
  1347 deg² (arXiv:2503.19439) [src: abstract].
- The SDSS page gives one date for DR11 and DR12. Cite DR12 for final BOSS.
- An arXiv identifier dates the paper, not the archive: the DESI DR1 paper
  (arXiv:2503.14745) and the archive opening coincide, the HSC PDR3 paper
  (arXiv:2108.13045) appeared the day before the PDR3 release date on the
  HSC release page (31 August 2021) [doc: https://hsc-release.mtk.nao.ac.jp/doc/, 2026-10-26].
