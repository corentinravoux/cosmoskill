# Weak lensing — current results and the history of S8

Scope: headline cosmic-shear, galaxy–galaxy-lensing, 3×2pt and 6×2pt
measurements, with the model assumed, and the history of the S8 number.
Snapshot: 2026-10-12

Flags: `[src]` read this session in the abstract or body of the cited paper;
`[sec]` taken from a named secondary source; `[der]` derived here, derivation
stated.
Unless a row says otherwise, S8 ≡ σ8 (Ωm/0.3)^0.5, the model is flat ΛCDM, and
errors are 68% intervals. Quoted values are the point estimates the papers put
in their abstracts. The DES abstracts state that they quote marginalised means
(arXiv:2105.13549, arXiv:2602.10065) [src]; the KiDS abstracts do not state
their estimator, and KiDS-1000 introduced an alternative to the marginal
estimate (arXiv:2007.01844) [src]. A comparison at the 0.5σ level therefore
needs the body of each paper. Read there on 2026-10-12: KiDS-Legacy quotes the
marginal mode with its highest-posterior-density interval, 0.815
+0.016/−0.021, from COSEBIs; the marginal mean is 0.813 ± 0.018 and the MAP
with its projected interval 0.811 +0.022/−0.015 (arXiv:2503.19441) [src: Sect.
5.1]. DES Y3 quotes the marginal mean with 34% intervals and the MAP beside it
(arXiv:2105.13543) [src: Sect. VII.6]; DES Y6 does the same (arXiv:2601.14559)
[src: Table 4]. A flag that names a place, as in `[src: Table 4]`, marks a
value read in the body of the paper.

The CMB reference in the DES Y6 papers is the combination of Planck 2018, ACT
DR6 and SPT-3G DR1 primary anisotropies (arXiv:2602.10065, arXiv:2601.14559)
[src]. The KiDS-Legacy comparison is with Planck Legacy alone
(arXiv:2503.19441) [src]. The two "tension" numbers therefore do not share a
reference and must not be put side by side without saying so. The values
behind them, read in the bodies: the DES Y6 combination has S8 = 0.836
+0.012/−0.013 and Ωm = 0.324 +0.007/−0.012 under the DES priors
(arXiv:2601.14559) [src: Table 4]; KiDS-Legacy compares with a Planck Legacy
marginal S8 = 0.834 ± 0.016 (arXiv:2503.19441) [src: Sect. 7]. The centres
agree; the DES reference is tighter. The 0.73σ of the KiDS-Legacy abstract is
the Hellinger distance in Σ8; in S8 the paper gives 0.76σ in its summary and
0.74σ in its appendix [src: Sect. 7, App. I.1.5].

More from the body of the DES Y6 papers: the cosmic-shear fits have p = 0.06
(NLA) and 0.13 (TATT) and the two posteriors differ by 0.6σ
(arXiv:2602.10065) [src: Sect. III, VII.3.1]; the TATT cosmic-shear mean 0.783
has a MAP of 0.763 (arXiv:2601.14559) [src: Table 4]. For HSC Y3 with DESI
redshifts, the body read here is the ar5iv rendering of an earlier version:
its abstract and Table 1 give 0.805 ± 0.018 (MAP 0.815) with the n(z)
calibrated on 0.3–3 h⁻¹Mpc and 0.804 +0.020/−0.019 (MAP 0.816) on 1–5
h⁻¹Mpc, with Ωm = 0.221 +0.033/−0.037 (arXiv:2511.18134) [src: Table 1 of
that rendering]; the current arXiv abstract gives the value of the table
above.

The DES Y6 cosmic-shear paper states that the NLA result is preferred by the
Bayesian evidence and that the choice of IA and baryon model can move S8 by up
to 1σ (arXiv:2602.10065) [src].

Read in the body of the DES Y6 3×2pt paper (arXiv:2601.14559): the 3×2pt MAP
is S8 = 0.793; with non-linear galaxy bias the mean is 0.789 ± 0.011; galaxy
clustering plus galaxy–galaxy lensing alone give 0.743 ± 0.023 (MAP 0.765)
and cosmic shear alone 0.783 +0.019/−0.015; 3×2pt + CMB gives 0.811 ± 0.008
[src: Table 4]. Against the CMB the difference is 2.6σ in S8, 2.2σ in the
S8–Ωm plane and 1.8σ in the full parameter space, and against Planck 2018
alone the full-parameter difference is 1.0σ, down from 1.5σ in Y3; the
best-constrained combination σ8(Ωm/0.3)^0.617 = 0.799 +0.011/−0.010 is 2.5σ
from the CMB [src: Sect. V.2]. The fiducial run uses TATT, linear galaxy bias
and no lens bin 2 [src: App. D, Sect. IV.8].

Earlier CMB-lensing combinations, for the history: DES Y3 3×2pt plus
cross-correlations with SPT + Planck CMB lensing gave S8 = 0.773 ± 0.016 and
Ωm = 0.344 ± 0.030, and S8 = 0.792 ± 0.012 once the CMB-lensing auto-spectrum
was added (Abbott et al. 2023, arXiv:2206.10824) [src]; the cross-correlations
alone gave S8 = 0.736 +0.032/−0.028 (Chang et al. 2023, arXiv:2203.12440)
[src]. ACT DR6 CMB lensing + BAO gives S8 = 0.840 ± 0.028, and with Planck
lensing 0.831 ± 0.023; that paper found the then-current KiDS, DES and HSC
values 1.7–2.1σ below CMB lensing under uniform assumptions (Madhavacheril et
al. 2024, arXiv:2304.05203) [src]. Detail belongs to
`cosmo-cmb-lss-cross-correlations`.

Extensions from DES Y3 3×2pt alone: (w_p, w_a) = (−0.99 +0.28/−0.17, −0.9 ±
1.2) at pivot z_p = 0.24, and Σ0 = 0.6 +0.4/−0.5 for the lensing-potential
modification; with external data (Σ0, μ0) = (0.04 ± 0.05, 0.08 +0.21/−0.19)
(DES Collaboration 2023, arXiv:2207.05766) [src]. See
`cosmo-dark-energy-modified-gravity`.

In w0waCDM the KiDS-Legacy + CMB-lensing combination gives a Bayes factor B =
0.07 for w0waCDM against ΛCDM, and with all probes B = 2.73, which the paper
equates to a 2.6σ suspiciousness (arXiv:2512.11041) [src]. Weak lensing
therefore does not drive the dynamical-dark-energy preference; see
`cosmo-dark-energy-modified-gravity`.

[src: Table 1, Sect. IV]: TATT with the DES scale cuts on the blue sample

### What supersedes what
<!-- key: what-supersedes-what -->

- **KiDS:** KiDS-450 (0.745) → KV450 (0.737) → KiDS-1000 (0.759) → KiDS-Legacy
  (0.815). The last step is a +0.056 shift in the central value [der: 0.815 −
  0.759], attributed by the collaboration mainly to redshift estimation and
  calibration (arXiv:2503.19441) [src]. Already in 2018 the KV450 paper noted
  that calibrating n(z) on COSMOS-2015 30-band photo-z, as DES and HSC then
  did, alleviated the tension (arXiv:1812.06076) [src]. The Legacy paper
  takes the step apart, in Hellinger distance to Planck (Σ8, then S8):
  KiDS-1000 rerun with the Legacy redshift estimation and calibration
  methods, 2.08σ and 1.42σ; with the Legacy bin limits and the larger KiDZ
  spectroscopic sample as well, 1.61σ and 1.12σ; KiDS-Legacy restricted to
  the KiDS-1000 footprint, five bins, NLA, 1.17σ and 0.85σ; adding the sixth
  bin and the NLA-M model there, 1.40σ and 1.00σ, with smaller errors; the
  full area, 0.73σ and 0.74σ, which the paper reads as a non-negligible
  statistical component (arXiv:2503.19441) [src: App. I.1.1–I.1.5].
- **DES:** Y1 (0.782) → Y3 fiducial (0.759) and optimised (0.772) → Y6 (0.798
  NLA, 0.783 TATT). The Y3 blending correction alone moved S8 by 0.5σ
  (arXiv:2105.13543) [src].
- **HSC:** Y1 (0.780 Cℓ, 0.823 ξ±) → Y3 (0.776 Cℓ, 0.769 ξ±; the ξ± abstract
  states wide flat redshift priors on the two highest bins) → Y3 recalibrated
  with DESI
  (0.804).
- **Joint:** DES Y3 + KiDS-1000 (0.790 mean, 0.801 MAP) is the reference
  "hybrid pipeline" result of 2023 and already showed that the mean and the
  MAP differ by more than half the error bar through projection effects
  (arXiv:2305.17173) [src].

The three HSC Y1/Y3 flavours are a standing example that two statistics on one
catalogue can differ: 0.780 (Cℓ) against 0.823 (ξ±) for Y1 [src:
arXiv:1809.09148, arXiv:1906.06041].
